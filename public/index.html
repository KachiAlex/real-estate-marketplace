What is PropertyArk?

PropertyArk is a real estate marketplace platform that enables users to browse, list, and transact properties across Nigeria. Our platform provides secure escrow services for property transactions, investment opportunities in real estate, mortgage facilitation, and professional property services. Users can search for properties by location, type, and price, connect with verified vendors, and complete transactions with confidence through our escrow protection.

Key Features

  • Browse thousands of property listings across Nigeria
  • Secure escrow services for safe property transactions
  • Real estate investment opportunities with verified returns
  • Mortgage facilitation and application management
  • Direct messaging with property vendors and agents
  • Property verification and KYC for trusted transactions

Browse Properties Without an Account

You can browse property listings, search by location and price, and view property details without creating an account. To list properties, make inquiries, use escrow services, or save favorites, please sign in or create a free account.
